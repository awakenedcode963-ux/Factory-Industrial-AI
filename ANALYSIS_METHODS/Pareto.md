# Pareto Analysis Methodology

## Purpose

This document defines how AI should apply Pareto Analysis (80/20 Principle) when analyzing manufacturing quality and production data.

The objective is not to produce Pareto charts.

The objective is to identify the small number of causes responsible for the majority of manufacturing losses and recommend where management should focus first.

Every Pareto analysis must end with management actions.

---

# Core Principle

Manufacturing resources are limited.

Management should not attempt to solve every problem simultaneously.

Instead, improvement efforts should focus on the few causes producing the greatest business impact.

Always ask

"What are the 20% of causes creating approximately 80% of the losses?"

---

# Analysis Sequence

The AI shall apply Pareto analysis in the following order.

---

## Step 1

Overall Factory Losses

Identify

- Total Scrap
- Total Rejects
- Total Purge
- Total Startup Scrap
- Total Burned
- Total Operational Loss

Determine which category contributes most to total losses.

---

## Step 2

Defect Pareto

Rank defects by

- Quantity
- Weight
- Scrap %
- Business Impact

Examples

Burned

Start & Restart

Half

Flash

Surface Scratch

Ovality

Diameter

Wall Thickness

Determine the few defects responsible for most losses.

---

## Step 3

Machine Pareto

Rank machines according to

Operational Scrap

Burned

Startup Scrap

Purge

Scrap per 1000 kg Produced

Never rank machines using total scrap only.

Normalize results first.

---

## Step 4

Product Pareto

Rank products according to

Operational Scrap

Reject Rate

Burned

Startup Scrap

Production Stability

Always separate

Injection Products

Extrusion Products

Socketing Products

Never mix different manufacturing processes.

---

## Step 5

Material Pareto

Separate analysis by raw material.

Examples

UPVC

PP-R

PP-H

PP-R UV

PP-R Multilayer

Never compare materials directly.

Determine which material family presents the largest operational improvement opportunity.

---

## Step 6

Crew Pareto

Rank production crews using

Operational Scrap

Burned

Startup Scrap

Process Stability

Never use engineering scrap for crew ranking.

---

## Step 7

Shift Pareto

Evaluate

Day Shift

Night Shift

Compare

Operational Scrap

Burned

Startup Losses

Reject Rate

Determine whether differences are operational or engineering.

---

## Step 8

Production Line Pareto

Evaluate

Injection Hall

Extrusion Hall

Socketing Department

Determine which department offers the highest improvement opportunity.

---

# Business Interpretation

Pareto results should never stop at ranking.

For every major contributor explain

Why it appears

Why it matters

What causes it

How it affects production

How it affects cost

Which department should act

Expected benefit after improvement

---

# Engineering Interpretation

Always distinguish between

Engineering Losses

Examples

Runner

Gate

Designed Overflow

Operational Losses

Examples

Purge

Burned

Startup Scrap

Restart Scrap

Improvement efforts should focus first on operational losses.

---

# Hidden Pareto

Do not perform Pareto using only one variable.

Also analyze

Machine × Burned

Machine × Startup

Machine × Product

Machine × Material

Crew × Startup

Crew × Burned

Product × Burned

Product × Machine

Material × Burned

Material × Startup

These combinations often reveal hidden opportunities.

---

# Executive Prioritization

Every Pareto result shall classify findings into

Critical Priority

Immediate Action Required

High Priority

Action within one week

Medium Priority

Monitor and improve

Low Priority

Routine monitoring only

---

# Executive Output

Every Pareto report shall include

Top Contributors

Contribution %

Cumulative %

Engineering Interpretation

Business Impact

Recommended Actions

Expected Savings

Responsible Department

Implementation Priority

---

# Decision Rules

Never recommend improving all machines.

Never recommend improving all products.

Never distribute resources equally.

Always concentrate improvement efforts where the greatest operational benefit exists.

---

# Golden Rules

Always apply Pareto separately for

Defects

Machines

Products

Materials

Crews

Departments

Never mix engineering scrap with operational scrap.

Always translate Pareto results into management actions.

A Pareto chart without decisions has no business value.
