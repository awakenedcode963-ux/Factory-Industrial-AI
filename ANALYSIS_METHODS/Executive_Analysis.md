# Executive Analysis Methodology

## Purpose

This document defines how the AI should analyze manufacturing quality data from an executive management perspective.

The objective is not to describe the data but to identify where management should act first to reduce manufacturing losses, improve process stability and increase profitability.

Every conclusion must be supported by data.

Never generate assumptions without evidence.

---

# Executive Thinking Principles

The AI must always think in the following order:

1. What is happening?

2. Why is it happening?

3. Where is it happening?

4. What is the business impact?

5. What should management do first?

The report must answer management questions rather than simply display statistics.

---

# Executive Priorities

The analysis should always prioritize:

1. Safety

2. Product Quality

3. Raw Material Loss

4. Process Stability

5. Machine Performance

6. Productivity

7. Standardization

---

# Manufacturing Philosophy

Not all scrap has the same value.

Engineering Scrap

Examples:

- Runner
- Gate
- Designed Overflow

These losses are expected because of mold design.

The objective is efficient recycling, not elimination.

Operational Scrap

Examples:

- Purge
- Startup Scrap
- Restart Scrap
- Burned Material
- Wrong Machine Settings

These losses are avoidable.

Management attention should focus here.

---

# Executive Analysis Flow

The AI shall analyze data in this order.

## Step 1

Understand the production process.

Never analyze numbers without understanding the manufacturing flow.

---

## Step 2

Validate data quality.

Examples:

Missing values

Wrong units

Duplicated records

Unknown codes

No Defect assigned to rejected products

Any abnormal pattern must be reported before analysis.

---

## Step 3

Measure overall factory performance.

Examples:

Acceptance Rate

Reject Rate

Scrap Weight

Scrap %

Production Quantity

---

## Step 4

Separate losses into categories.

Engineering Loss

Operational Loss

Quality Loss

Unknown Loss

---

## Step 5

Apply Pareto Analysis.

Identify the small number of causes generating most losses.

Focus improvement efforts on those causes.

---

## Step 6

Locate where the losses occur.

Machine

Product

Material

Production Line

Shift

Crew

Inspector

Date

Time

---

## Step 7

Search for hidden relationships.

Examples:

Machine × Product

Machine × Material

Machine × Defect

Crew × Startup Scrap

Shift × Burned

Material × Reject Rate

Product × Machine

---

## Step 8

Estimate business impact.

Examples:

Production Loss

Material Waste

Machine Downtime

Reduced Productivity

Customer Risk

Additional Labor

---

## Step 9

Recommend actions.

Every recommendation must include

Problem

Evidence

Root Cause

Recommended Action

Responsible Department

Expected Benefit

Implementation Priority

---

# Report Structure

The final report should always include

Executive Summary

Major Findings

Critical Risks

Business Opportunities

Immediate Actions

Quick Wins

Long-Term Improvements

Management Conclusion

---

# Writing Style

Use executive language.

Avoid technical complexity unless necessary.

Focus on business decisions.

Do not overload management with tables.

Explain what the numbers mean.

Translate statistics into actions.

---

# Golden Rule

The objective is not to produce a beautiful report.

The objective is to help management make better decisions using manufacturing data.

Every page should answer one management question.
