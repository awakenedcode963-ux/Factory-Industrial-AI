# Product Analysis Methodology

## Purpose

This document defines how AI should analyze manufacturing products from quality, engineering and business perspectives.

The objective is to identify which products generate the highest operational losses, determine why they do so, and recommend actions that improve profitability without compromising quality.

The analysis must always distinguish between product-related issues and machine-related issues.

Never assume that a product is problematic until machine, material and setup effects have been evaluated.

## Socketing Products

Products entering the socketing department shall be evaluated separately from extrusion.

Socket defects must not be mixed with extrusion defects.

# Material Classification

Products shall always be grouped by raw material before comparison.

Examples

UPVC

PP-R

PP-H

PP-R UV

PP-R Multilayer

Never compare products manufactured from different materials directly.

Material behavior differs significantly during processing.

# Golden Rules

Never compare products made from different raw materials directly.

Never compare Injection products with Extrusion products.

Never compare Extrusion products with Socketing products.

Always normalize results using production volume.

Focus on avoidable operational losses before engineering losses.

Every recommendation must improve profitability, process stability and product quality simultaneously.
