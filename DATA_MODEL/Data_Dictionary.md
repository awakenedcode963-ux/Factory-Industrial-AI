# Data Dictionary

## Purpose

This document defines the business meaning of every important field used in the manufacturing quality database.

The AI shall understand the meaning of each field before performing any analysis.

Never rely only on column names.

Always understand the business purpose behind each field.

---

# Manufacturing Departments

The factory consists of two primary production departments.

## Injection Department

Produces plastic fittings.

Processes

UPVC

PP-R

PP-H

Typical Products

Elbows

Tees

Reducers

Caps

Sockets

Couplings

Adaptors

Unions

Crosses

Other injection molded fittings

---

## Extrusion Department

Produces plastic pipes.

Processes

UPVC

PP-R

PP-R UV

PP-R Multilayer

PP-H

After extrusion, some pipe products continue to the Socketing process.

## Accepted Quantity

Injection

Pieces

---

## Rejected Quantity

Quantity rejected because of defects.

Never include engineering losses.

---

## Scrap Weight

Total measured scrap weight.

Normally measured in

Kilograms

or

Grams

Always verify units before calculations.

---

## Runner Weight

Engineering Scrap.

Generated by mold design.

Normally unavoidable.

Must be reported separately.

Never classify Runner as operational loss.

---

## Purge Weight

Material discarded during cleaning.

Operational Scrap.

Normally avoidable.

One of the highest-value improvement opportunities.

---

## Burned Scrap

Material damaged because of thermal degradation.

Critical quality indicator.

Usually linked with

Machine setup

Residence Time

Temperature

Purge

Startup

---

## Startup Scrap

Material lost before stable production.

Operational loss.

Includes

Start Working

Restart Working

Machine setup stabilization

Always analyze separately.

---

# Business Rule

The AI must always distinguish between

Engineering Scrap

Operational Scrap

Quality Scrap

Management Losses

Never report Total Scrap without separating these categories.

This separation is essential for accurate decision making.
