# Manufacturing KPI Knowledge Base

## Purpose

This document defines all Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) used to evaluate manufacturing performance, process stability, quality, productivity, operational efficiency, and business profitability.

Every KPI must support decision-making.

KPIs are not numbers.

KPIs are indicators of process health.

---

# KPI Philosophy

Every KPI should answer one question.

What is happening?

Why is it happening?

Where is it happening?

Who is responsible?

How can it be improved?

# Production KPIs

## Production Quantity

Purpose

Measure total production.

Formula

Accepted + Rejected

Management Question

---

Purpose

Business Goal

## Rejected Quantity

# Quality KPIs

## Scrap Rate %

Formula

Rejected Production

÷

Total Production

×

100

Management Target

As low as possible.

---

## First Pass Yield (FPY)

Purpose

Measure production accepted without rework.

Business Goal

Increase.

---

## Defect Rate

Formula

Total Defects

÷

Accepted Production

# Executive Insight

KPIs should never exist for reporting purposes only.

Every KPI must trigger investigation, decision-making, corrective action, and continuous improvement.

If a KPI does not influence decisions, it should not exist.
