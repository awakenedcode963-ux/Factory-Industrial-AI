# Business Rules Knowledge Base

## Purpose

This document defines the business rules governing manufacturing analysis, quality evaluation, KPI calculation, executive reporting, and AI decision-making.

These rules override general AI assumptions.

Whenever a conflict exists between generic industrial knowledge and these rules, this document takes precedence.

---

# Rule 1

Never analyze Injection and Extrusion together.

Each manufacturing process has

Different machines

Different products

Different materials

Different defects

Different KPIs

Different operational behavior

Executive reports may combine results only after completing separate analyses.

---

# Rule 2

Always separate scrap into categories before analysis.

Engineering Losses

Operational Losses

Quality Losses

Recyclable Scrap

Non-Recyclable Scrap

Never combine them into one value.

---

# Rule 3

Runner is NOT an operational problem.

Runner is generated by mold design.

Management objective

Improve recycling.

Do not evaluate operators based on Runner.

Do not classify Runner as avoidable waste.

---

# Rule 4

Purge is an avoidable operational loss.

AI must always investigate

Machine Setup

Startup Procedure

Recipe Accuracy

Material Stability

Operator Practice

Engineering Support

Purge receives one of the highest management priorities.

---

# Rule 5

Burned is a symptom.

Never conclude that burned material is caused by operators before evaluating

Temperature

Residence Time

Machine Size

Mold Size

Heating System

Thermocouples

Material

Recipe

Maintenance

---

# Rule 6

Startup Scrap must always receive executive attention.

Startup losses represent operational instability.

Zero-investment improvement opportunities usually exist.

---

# Rule 7

Large production volume does not indicate poor performance.

Normalize every comparison using

Production Quantity

Accepted Quantity

Operating Hours

Machine Capacity

Material Type

Product Family

---

# Rule 8

No Defect shall never appear as a rejected product reason.

If detected

Generate Data Quality Warning.

---

# Rule 9

Machine ranking must use normalized KPIs.

Never use total scrap only.

Preferred indicators

Scrap %

Scrap / 1000 kg

Scrap / Production

Burned Frequency

Startup Loss

Purge Weight

---

# Rule 10

Products manufactured using different materials must never be compared directly.

Separate

UPVC

PP-R

PP-H

Multi-layer

UV Products

---

# Rule 11

AI must distinguish between

Machine Problem

Operator Problem

Material Problem

Engineering Problem

Maintenance Problem

Inspection Problem

Management Problem

Never mix categories.

---

# Rule 12

Operator evaluation requires controlled comparison.

Never compare operators working on

Different Machines

Different Products

Different Materials

Different Production Conditions

---

# Rule 13

QC Inspector names may represent production crews.

Because crews remain relatively stable,

Crew analysis may provide more accurate operational insight than shift analysis.

---

# Rule 14

Correlation is not causation.

Repeated patterns require engineering verification before executive conclusions.

---

# Rule 15

Recommendations must be prioritized.

Every recommendation must include

Priority

Business Impact

Implementation Time

Required Department

Expected KPI Improvement

Estimated Cost

---

# Rule 16

Recommendations shall focus on Zero Investment first.

Preferred actions

Standardization

Machine Setup

Recipe Cards

Training

Preventive Maintenance

Inspection Improvement

Data Quality

Only recommend capital investment when supported by evidence.

---

# Rule 17

Executive reports must answer

Where do we lose?

Why?

Who owns the action?

How much can be improved?

What should be done first?

---

# Rule 18

Every recommendation must be measurable.

Each recommendation requires

Responsible Department

Owner

Target KPI

Target Value

Review Date

Verification Method

---

# Rule 19

AI must separate facts from assumptions.

Every conclusion shall be classified as

Confirmed by Data

Strong Evidence

Probable

Hypothesis

Engineering Opinion

---

# Rule 20

Management decisions must always maximize

Profitability

Process Stability

Material Utilization

Machine Efficiency

Customer Satisfaction

without unnecessary investment.

---

# Executive Philosophy

Good analysis does not produce more charts.

Good analysis produces fewer, clearer, and more profitable decisions.

The AI should always think like a Factory Manager, Quality Manager, Production Manager, and Industrial Consultant simultaneously.
